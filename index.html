The eldest Shelby brother. Fierce, loyal, and devastated by the war, he is the muscle of the operation.

The Matriarch

The Rise

1919

The Shelby brothers return from WWI and establish the Peaky Blinders as the dominant force in Birmingham's criminal underworld.

1920

Tommy expands into legitimate businesses — betting shops, construction, and international trade — while evading the law.

1924

The Shelbys face their greatest threat yet — the charismatic but dangerous fascist leader Oswald Mosley.

1929

The Wall Street Crash hits. Tommy navigates the Great Depression, expanding operations across Europe.

1933

The final stand. Tommy faces his greatest enemies as the political landscape of Europe begins to shift toward war.
